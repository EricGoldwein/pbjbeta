Methodology & Definitions
Staff Categories
  • Total Staff: All nursing staff including RNs, LPNs, CNAs, administrators, and DON
  • Direct Staff: Staff providing direct patient care (excludes administrators and DON)
  • Total RN (Registered Nurse): Includes RNs + RN administrators + RN DON (Director of Nursing)
  • Direct RN: Excludes RN Admin, RN DON.
  • LPN (Licensed Practical Nurse): Includes direct care LPNs + LPN administrators
  • Nurse Aides: Includes CNAs + NA trainees + Medication Aides
  • Contract Staff: Temporary/agency staff (marked with "_ctr" suffix)
Calculations
  • HPRD (Hours Per Resident Day): Total hours ÷ Census
  • Contract %: (Contract hours ÷ Total hours) × 100
  • Averages: Weighted by census when applicable
  • Rounding: Financial rounding (ROUND_HALF_UP) for all displayed metrics
  • SER (Staffing-to-Expected Ratio) or % Case-Mix: Reported ÷ CMS Case-Mix. Values below 1.0 indicate staffing deficit relative to CMS benchmark expectations; values above 1.0 indicate surplus staffing. Case-mix is a CMS benchmark for staffing based on resident acuity.
Statistical Analysis
  • Z-Score: (Value - Mean) ÷ Standard Deviation
  • Outlier Detection: |Z-Score| ≥ 2.0 (High), |Z-Score| ≥ 3.0 (Extreme)
  • Percentile: Approximate ranking compared to historical data
  • Holidays: Federal holidays as defined by U.S. government
Data Sources

This Nursing Home Staffing Dashboard uses CMS Payroll-Based Journal (PBJ) data from 2017 to 2025, covering all nursing positions, including contract staff (https://data.cms.gov/quality-of-care/payroll-based-journal-daily-nurse-staffing). CMS first published PBJ data in 2017. It also uses Provider Information datasets from 2018 to 2025 (https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/4pq5-n9py).

Unlike PBJ data, which is released quarterly, provider info files are published on a rolling monthly basis without a clear reference quarter. To align them with PBJ datasets, we matched the "reported total staffing" metric, which is available in both sources, to the corresponding quarter. This approach is not exact—health inspection data, for example, is updated on a different schedule—but it offers a reasonable best-guess for quarter assignment. Note: Case-Mix data from Q4 2023 is not available.
