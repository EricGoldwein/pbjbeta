Headcount Methodology

Bars are distinct SYS_EMPLEE_ID with positive hours in each time bucket and CMS job code (role). That matches how CMS stores Employee Detail; it is the right view for role mix.

Because someone can work two roles in the same period, the same person can appear in more than one stack segment. Summing bar heights can overstate unique people. A single unique-person series would need a rule (e.g. primary role = most hours in the period)—that is a different metric; this chart keeps CMS job codes explicit.

Nurse tab = licensed and direct-care nursing codes 5–12 (RN DON 5, RN admin 6, RN 7, LPN admin 8, LPN 9, CNA 10, aide in training 11, med aide 12). Non-nurse = all other codes in the extract; All = every code.

Contract in a role = EMP_CTR = 2 with hours in that bucket. The optional contract trend line is deduped contract staff in the period (any role). Tooltips on bars show both staff and contract for that role.
