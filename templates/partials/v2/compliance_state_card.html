California staffing law excerpt (HPRD):
• 3.50 HPRD (direct care service hours): Each facility shall employ sufficient nursing staff to provide a minimum of 3.50 direct care service hours per patient day (except as set forth in HSC 1276.9). Skilled nursing facilities shall provide a minimum of 2.4 CNA hours per patient day to meet the requirements.
• 0.06 HPRD: Nursing service personnel shall be employed and on duty in at least the number and with the qualifications determined by the Department; staffing requirements are minimum standards only. SNFs shall employ and schedule additional staff as needed for quality resident care and compliance with relevant state and federal staffing requirements.
- (b) Facilities licensed for 59 or fewer beds: at least one RN or LVN, awake and on duty, in the facility at all times (day and night).
- (c) Facilities licensed for 60 to 99 beds: at least one RN or LVN, awake and on duty, in addition to the DNS (DNS shall not have charge nurse responsibilities).
• 0.24 HPRD (of total) / 2.40 HPRD (of total): Facilities licensed for 100 or more beds: at least one RN, awake and on duty, in addition to the DNS (DNS shall not have charge nurse responsibilities).
Source: MACPAC for state policies related to nursing home staffing.
