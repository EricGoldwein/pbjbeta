    PBJ320 AI sends your export and structured instructions to a third-party model (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini). The starter prompt and Claude add-on are built to work from this facility’s data — but the model can still misread the file or sound more certain than the sources support.
    
                
    
                
    Before you rely on the output
    
                
      
                    
    • Spot-check key numbers on this dashboard and in the CSV against CMS payroll and related public files.
      • 
                    
    • Use the starter prompt (and Claude add-on when available) so the model follows PBJ320’s review structure.
      • 
                    
    • Do not paste resident-identifiable or privileged material you are not allowed to share with that service.
      • 
                    
    • Treat AI output as a draft for your review — not a substitute for counsel, clinical judgment, or CMS filings.
      • 
                
    
                
    PBJ payroll data describe reported staffing only. Careful prompts improve reliability; they do not guarantee the model will never err or hallucinate.
