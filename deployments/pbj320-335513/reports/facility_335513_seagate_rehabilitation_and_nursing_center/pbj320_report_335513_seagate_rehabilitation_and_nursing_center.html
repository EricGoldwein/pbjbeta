New York State Staffing Requirements

New York State Staffing Requirements: 3.56 HPRD (Source: MACPAC - Medicaid and CHIP Payment and Access Commission)

New York State Public Health Law Section 2803-d - Minimum Staffing Requirements

New York State requires nursing homes to maintain minimum staffing levels to ensure adequate care for residents. The state has established specific HPRD (Hours Per Resident Day) requirements that facilities must meet.

Requirement HPRD Description
Minimum Total Staffing 3.56 HPRD New York State requires nursing homes to maintain a minimum of 3.56 hours per resident day of total nursing staff (RN, LPN, and CNA combined). This includes both direct care staff and administrative staff.
RN Staffing Minimum Required Facilities must maintain adequate RN staffing to meet resident care needs. RN hours include both direct care RNs and RN administrators/DON.
CNA Staffing Minimum Required Certified Nurse Aide staffing must be sufficient to provide direct care services to all residents.

Enforcement: Facilities that fail to meet New York State minimum staffing requirements may be subject to penalties, fines, and enforcement actions by the New York State Department of Health.

Source: New York State Public Health Law Section 2803-d | MACPAC State Staffing Standards Database
