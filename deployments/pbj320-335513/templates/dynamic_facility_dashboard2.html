{% if show_ein_position_section %}8.{% else %}7.{% endif %} Methodology{% if show_ein_position_section %}8.{% else %}7.{% endif %} Methodology and Sources

Definitions and calculations
Staff Categories
  • Total Staff: All nursing staff including RNs, LPNs, CNAs, RN/LPN administrators, and DON
  • Direct Staff: Staff providing direct patient care (excludes administrators and DON)
  • Total RN (Registered Nurse): Includes RNs + RN administrators + RN DON (Director of Nursing)
  • Direct RN: Excludes RN Admin, RN DON.
  • LPN (Licensed Practical Nurse): Includes direct care LPNs + LPN administrators
  • Nurse Aides: Includes CNAs + NA trainees + Medication Aides
  • Contract Staff: Temporary/agency staff (marked with "_ctr" suffix)
Calculations
  • HPRD (Hours Per Resident Day): Total hours ÷ Census
  • Contract Staff %: (Contract hours ÷ Total hours) × 100
  • Averages: Weighted by census when applicable
  • Rounding: Financial rounding (ROUND_HALF_UP) for all displayed metrics
  • % Case-Mix HPRD: Reported ÷ CMS Case-Mix. Values below 1.0 indicate a potential staffing deficit relative to CMS benchmark expectations; values above 1.0 indicate surplus staffing. Case-mix is a CMS benchmark for staffing based on resident acuity.
  • Non-Direct Staff: RN Admin/DON & LPN Admin hours per resident day
Harrington Expected Staffing

The Harrington Expected formulas adjust expected staffing based on Case-Mix Index (CMI) to account for resident acuity:

  • Total Expected HPRD: 3.48 + ((CMI - 0.62) / (3.84 - 0.62))0.715 × (7.68 - 3.48)
  • RN Expected HPRD: 0.55 + ((CMI - 0.62) / (3.84 - 0.62))0.974 × (2.39 - 0.55)
  • Nurse Aide Expected HPRD: 2.45 + ((CMI - 0.62) / (3.84 - 0.62))0.236 × (3.6 - 2.45)

See: Nursing Home Guide to Adjusting Nurse Staffing for Resident Case-Mix (Harrington)

Note: Direct Care metrics are calculated from PBJ and exclude Admin/DON.

Statistical Analysis
  • Z-Score: (Value - Mean) ÷ Standard Deviation
  • Outlier Detection: |Z-Score| ≥ 2.0 (High), |Z-Score| ≥ 3.0 (Extreme)
  • Percentile: Approximate ranking compared to historical data
  • Holidays: Federal holidays as defined by U.S. government

Roster = codes 1 + 5–12.

Role position numbers (#1, #2, …): For each job code, employees are numbered in order of first work day in the loaded Employee Detail file for that role (then tie-break by employee ID). #1 = earliest first appearance for that code; several people each keep their own #. In All quarters roster view, rows are grouped person×role (employee-first), and Active means that person×role appears in the most recent quarter present in the loaded file. Labels are for readability only. Not inspection citations: Deficiency tags, Form 2567 statement numbers, and similar citation labels come from a different CMS system and are unrelated to these roster numbers or job codes.

Cross-check in roster modal: PBJ daily column sum vs Employee Detail code-sum for that date. Warning threshold is ~±2.5 h or ~3%. Direct RN compares Hrs_RN vs code 7; total RN compares Hrs_RN + Hrs_RNadmin + Hrs_RNDON vs codes 5+6+7.
